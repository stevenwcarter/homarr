Hover or focus the widget to pause its slideshow; touch devices keep the control available. A paused slideshow keeps its resume control visible. The advanced view displays the complete photo without cropping over the chosen backdrop, keeps pause or resume in its corner, and includes every loaded photo in its thumbnail strip. With navigation arrows enabled, previous and next appear on the sides of the photo in both views while the pointer is over the widget; otherwise the advanced view groups previous, pause or resume, and next together. If an Immich refresh fails, the last loaded album stays visible with a stale-data warning.

For a selected album, its first photo can appear while the remaining photos load. Once the full album arrives, every photo remains available to the slideshow and advanced thumbnail strip. If randomization is enabled, the preview photo stays first and the remaining photos follow a random order that stays the same while the tab is open. The current photo and the paused state are also kept while the tab is open, including across edit mode.
